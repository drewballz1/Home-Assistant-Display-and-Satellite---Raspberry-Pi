# TD2 satellite stand — on-site fixes (after the prints arrive)

No test prints, so every fit-critical feature can be adjusted by hand. Do these before assembly.

## Tools
- Drill bits: **1/16"** and **3/32"** (a pin vise or a drill on low speed)
- Hobby knife, fine sandpaper (220–400), a dab of hot glue

## Holes (printed undersize on purpose)
| Where | Printed | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| Screw pilots: 4 Pi + 2 ReSpeaker standoffs (stand), 4 in the speaker box | Ø1.4 mm | Drill **1/16"** to the bottom of each standoff/pilot. Don't go through the back wall: the standoffs are only 5 mm (Pi) and 3 mm (ReSpeaker) tall. |
| 4 speaker screw holes, back of the stand | Ø2.4 mm through, Ø4.4 recess | If an M2 screw won't pass, drill **3/32"** through. Leave the recess as printed. |
| 2 mic pinholes, back wall | Ø1.5 mm | Drill **1/16"** by hand, straight through. Don't go bigger: the foam seal relies on a small hole. |
| USR poke hole | Ø2.6 mm | Clear with a pin if strung. |

## Moving / snap parts
| Feature | Check | Fix |
|---|---|---|
| **MUTE button** (U-shaped slot) | Press it: the tab should flex and spring back. | Run a hobby knife through the slot to clear any skin or strings. Never glue or fill it. |
| **Frame on the stand** (10 snap tabs) | The frame should click on firmly. The frame is PETG and the stand ASA, which shrink slightly differently. | **Tight:** sand the inside of the frame where it rubs, lightly, a few strokes at a time. Don't sand the stand's tabs. **Loose:** a strip of thin tape on the stand's rim, or 2–3 dots of hot glue inside the frame corners. |
| **Pi ports cover** | It should clip into the side opening and sit flush. | **Tight:** sand the cover's rim (the cover is the easy part to sand). **Loose:** a small piece of tape on the rim. |
| Upper 2 Pi standoffs | Tops should be flat. | Trim any whisker on the top edge with the knife so the Pi sits flat. |

## Before closing it up
- Display glass edge: measure it. Use **2 mm foam tape** on the spacer ring (it covers 5.95–6.95 mm glass).
- Speaker: check the 4 mounting slots line up with the 4 screw holes (designed for a 60 mm square).
- ReSpeaker: confirm the button nearest the plain end (no connectors) is the one marked MUTE.
